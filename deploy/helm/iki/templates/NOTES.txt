Iki {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is deployed as release "{{ .Release.Name }}" in namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

Check the rollout:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "iki.fullname" . }}-web

First install? Set up sign-in with a one-time link (valid for 60 minutes):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "iki.fullname" . }}-web -- iki-server setup_link
Open the link it prints and follow the wizard. It connects your identity
provider; whoever signs in at the end becomes the first administrator.

Sign-in broken later (for example after changing identity providers)?
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "iki.fullname" . }}-web -- iki-server setup_link --recover

Readiness (database reachable, migrations applied):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "iki.fullname" . }}-web 8000:8000
  curl http://localhost:8000/healthz/ready
