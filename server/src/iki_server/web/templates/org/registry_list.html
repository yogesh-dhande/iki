Registries

A registry keeps identities: each compound, sequence or antibody it has is registered once, with one ID, such as CPD-000123, which every project in it that has the thing shares. Projects keep their own data about it. A project is in one registry, chosen when it's made. Registering something another project in the registry has tells you it's there and its ID, never who has it or what they know about it. To keep a client's or partner's identities apart even from that, give them a registry of their own.
