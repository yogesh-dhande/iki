## In short

The example is a pUC19 prep sequenced on a MinION: {v.reads_total} public reads (BioSample
{puc19.data.biosample}, every 900th read of SRA run {puc19.data.run}), checked against pUC19's
record, GenBank {puc19.data.accession} ({number(v.reference_length)} bp, {v.topology}). The
results below are the plasmid sequencing plugin's for these reads.

The deployment needs the three plugins and a worker bound to the **long-read tools** environment.
The prep is a **Plasmid prep** record in the lots plugin, made from the plasmid your lab
registered, and the analyst and reviewer have the lots plugin's **Verification analyst** and
**Verification reviewer** roles in the project.

<BeforeYouStart prerequisites={frontmatter.prerequisites} />

<Step n={1} id="reads" title="Register the reads" role="Verification analyst">

Record the **Sequencing run**, the prep's **Sequencing library** and its **FASTQ read set** in the
sequencing plugin. Register the basecalled FASTQ where it already lies, in a bucket your admin has
approved: Iki records the file's version and checksum, and reads it from there.

Basecalling is done with your own licensed tool before this step.

</Step>

<Step n={2} id="verify" title="Verify the plasmid from its reads" role="Verification analyst">

Open the plasmid and run **Verify a plasmid from its long reads**. Choose the **Read set**, where
the reads are from (**nanopore** or **PacBio HiFi**), and the **Minimum depth** a base needs to be
called, 10 unless you change it.

The run makes a [**Whole-plasmid alignment**](/reference/plugins/plasmid-sequencing/#WholePlasmidAlignment) (WPA) on one of the lab's workers. If a sequencing
service sent a consensus instead, run **Verify a plasmid from a service's consensus** with its one
FASTA sequence and name the provider; the rest of this use case is the same.

</Step>

The first {mcs.length} sit in the multiple cloning site, which the record doesn't annotate as a

The alignment's **Map** tab shows read depth round the ring, the plasmid's features, and a mark at
each difference. Choose a layer to show or hide it, or read the same in its tables.

<Step n={4} id="record" title="Record the verification" role="Verification analyst">

Open the prep and run **Record plasmid verification**. Choose the alignment, and add an
**Externally reviewed report** if there is one. Record the **Outcome** the evidence supports,
**match**, **discrepancy** or **inconclusive**, with **Notes** that explain it, and assign the
**Reviewer**.

A match over an alignment that differs or is incomplete needs a **Reason for overriding the
verdict**. For this prep the outcome is **discrepancy**, with the note "Its multiple cloning site
differs from L09137.2, every read agreeing."

The verification records the plasmid's sequence as it was when it ran, so a later edit to the
plasmid doesn't change what it says.

</Step>

<Step n={5} id="sign" title="Sign the outcome" role="Verification reviewer, lot QA">

Signing locks the verification. Editing it afterwards returns it to **Recorded** for a new review,
and the earlier signatures stay in its history. New sequencing of the same prep is a new
verification.

Releasing the lot the prep belongs to is a separate step, in the lots plugin.

</Step>

## What Iki does not decide

Iki aligns the reads, calls the consensus and names each difference from the plasmid's sequence.
Whether a difference matters for what the prep is for, and so which outcome to record, is the
analyst's and reviewer's call, and a match over a differing alignment needs their stated reason.
