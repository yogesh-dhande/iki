A type whose records are things in the lab, such as a sample, a lot or a tube, can keep how much
of each there is: its [`amount`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount). Iki keeps it, never
below zero, and changes it only by its own operations: recorded when a record is made, then drawn
(used up), transferred to another record, split into aliquots, or adjusted to what it was found to
be, with a reason.

Each change is a movement of the record's ledger, with who made it, how, when and why; two people
drawing at once take turns. The amount isn't one of the record's values, so it isn't in its
revisions, and a state that locks the record doesn't stop it being drawn.

The Inventory plugin's tube is drawn by volume, or by mass at its concentration, and split into
tubes of its own type:

```yaml
name: Tube
amount:
  unit: uL
  label: Volume
  description: How much is left in the tube.
  concentration: concentration
  material: contents
  follows: [contents]
fields:
  contents: { type: ref, to: iki/core.Entity, immutable: true }
  concentration:
    type: quantity
    unit: mg/mL
    min: 0
    description: Mass per unit volume, when known.
```

## Units and concentrations

- [`unit`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.unit) measures how much there is of
  something: a volume, a mass, an amount of substance or a count such as `{vial}`, never a
  temperature or a ratio (`amount-unit`). An amount is given in its unit or any that converts to
  it, and kept exactly, as a decimal; each movement keeps what was asked for as it was given, to 40
  places either side of its point.
  [`display_unit`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.display_unit) measures the same
  thing (`amount-unit`).
- [`whole_units: true`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.whole_units) requires a whole
  number in the ledger's unit after conversion, for a lot of individual consumables: its starting
  stock, draws, transfers, splits and adjustments. The inventory plugin's count lots:
  `amount: { unit: "1", label: Consumable count, material: item, whole_units: true }`.
- [`concentration`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.concentration) names a quantity
  field of the type whose unit, times a volume, is a mass or an amount of substance (mg/mL,
  mmol/L), or the other way about (`amount-concentration`). An amount in a volume is then drawn as
  a mass at the record's concentration, and an amount in a mass as a volume: 50 ug of a 2 mg/mL
  sample is 25 uL.

## Splits, transfers and what they're of

- A split makes each aliquot a new record in the same project, holding its share, and takes
  exactly what they hold: nothing is made or lost, and it's all of them or none. An aliquot is of
  the type's own type by default, with its values but not its unique ones or its states. Or
  [`aliquots`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.aliquots) names another type with an
  amount in the same kind of unit, and [`of`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.aliquots.of)
  its reference to the type, which names the record each was split from (`amount-aliquots`).
- Each aliquot depends on what it was split from, and a record transferred into depends on what
  was moved into it. A draw with `used_in` links the receiving record to the stock it used.
  Lineage walks can follow or filter these edges as `aliquot`, `transfer` or `used`.
- [`material`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.material) names an immutable reference
  of the type to what its amount is of, such as a lot's catalogue item (`amount-material`). An
  empty container can leave it unset and name its contents when it's first filled, but a record
  with a positive amount must name its material. A transfer into one of its records takes only material of that entity, from
  a record whose type says so too (a reference to a registry entry and one to a project's record of
  it are the same entity), and its aliquots are of it too, whatever they're given. Pooling different
  materials makes a mixture: a record of its own.
- A transfer into a record a state locks, such as a released lot, takes only material from a
  record in that same state, so nothing that wasn't released becomes part of what was.
- [`follows`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#amount.follows) names references whose records'
  eligibility a draw also meets, such as a tube's lot: a state holding the lot's amount holds the
  tube's, and the lot's draw guards are checked too. The Inventory plugin's tubes follow
  `[contents]`.

## Holds and locks

[`holds_amount: true`](/reference/plugin-spec/field/#states.holds_amount) on a state stops any of
the amount being drawn, transferred or split off while the record's in it, as a quarantine does. It
can still be adjusted. Only a type with an amount has a state that holds it
(`holds-without-amount`), or an abstract type whose subtypes each keep their own, in their own
units, as every kind of lot shares one disposition; a concrete subtype of it without an amount is
refused.

Draws can be narrowed further:

- [`draw_guards`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_guards) are conditions on taking an
  amount, by a draw, a transfer or a split, in CEL as guards are. They read `record`, `now`,
  `today`, `operation` and `purpose`, and each subtype keeps its ancestors'.
- [`draw_purposes`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_purposes) names the lab roles that may
  draw for each purpose, `routine` or `qa_sampling`, in the record's project. Routine draws are
  open to Editors unless restricted, and QA sampling requires a declaration.

## Receiving a draw into a record

A destination type can record each draw made into it: declare
[`draws_received`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draws_received) naming a computed JSON
field among its fields, such as `material_draws: { type: json, computed: true }`.

- Each draw whose `used_in` is the record appends the movement's ID, the drawn record's ID and
  revision, the positive quantity in the ledger's unit, the person and the time. A tube's upstream
  lot ID and revision are included when the person can refer to that lot in the receiving project.
- The debit and the receiving record's revision commit together. The person must be able to edit
  the destination and draw from the material, and a state lock on the destination prevents the
  draw.
- Signatures cover the receipts on the revision they sign, and subtypes inherit the field.

A destination can also take its one draw from the record assigned to it, while it waits in a
state: declare [`draw_target`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target). The Samples
plugin's sample takes its draw from its source tube or lot while it's awaiting the draw:

```yaml
draw_target: { from: source, state: { field: status, is: pending } }
draws_received: receipt
```

- [`from`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.from) names the required, immutable
  reference to the container the draw must come from, and
  [`state`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.state) the state the destination must
  be in. One draw can be recorded against that source while the destination is in it, and the
  debit and the destination's receipt commit together.
- A prepared draw also fixes the upstream
  [`source`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.source), such as a lot, the planned
  [`amount`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.amount), the computed
  [`ready`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.ready) field that must be set first,
  and the lab roles, [`by`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.by), that may record
  it. These four go together.
- [`stored: true`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#draw_target.stored) requires the container
  to be in its storage position before the draw.

## One amount per record

A record has one amount: a subtype has its parent's (`amount-twice`). A type with an identity has
none, since each project has one record of each entry, which isn't a thing in the lab
(`amount-on-identity`).

The REST API's `POST /api/v1/entities` takes an `amount`; `/entities/{ref}/amount` draws,
transfers and adjusts it and lists its ledger, and `/entities/{ref}/aliquots` splits it. Each
change is in the audit log, and the webhook event `entity.amount_moved` says so.

## Where things are kept

A type with [`storage`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#storage) has records that are kept
somewhere, or that keep others. Iki keeps where each is, in which container and position, who has
it, and every move, as a ledger of its own. The inventory plugin's stock location is a shelf or
rack of lots:

```yaml
name: StockLocation
label: Stock location
schema_version: 1
hrid: { prefix: SLC }
storage:
  holds: [StockLot]
  grid: { rows: rows, columns: columns, labels: letter_number, order: by_row }
fields:
  name: { type: string, required: true, max_length: 160, indexed: true }
  rows: { type: int, required: true, immutable: true, min: 1, max: 26, default: 1 }
  columns: { type: int, required: true, immutable: true, min: 1, max: 48, default: 12 }
```

- [`holds`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#storage.holds) names the types that can be kept
  in it, and [`grid`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#storage.grid) its positions in rows and
  columns, each holding one record.
- [`barcode`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#storage.barcode) names a unique string field
  holding the code on it already, and
  [`confirm`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#storage.confirm) what needs that code scanned
  first, a `draw` or a `move`. The Inventory plugin's tubes are scanned before each draw.
- A type kept in storage declares `storage: {}`, as the Reagents plugin's stock lots do.

## Stock and reorders

A catalogue type opts into the project's stock view and reorder list with
[`stock_catalogue`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue). A lab declares its item,
lot, order and recipient fields on its own types. The inventory plugin's stock item:

```yaml
name: StockItem
hrid: { prefix: SCI }
stock_catalogue:
  name: name
  unit: stock_unit
  threshold: low_stock_at
  notify: [stock_manager]
  lots: { type: StockLot, item: item, number: lot_number, expiry: expires_on }
  orders:
    {
      type: ReorderLine,
      item: item,
      ordered: ordered_packs,
      received: received_packs,
      reference: order_reference,
    }
fields:
  stock_unit:
    type: enum
    values: [mg, mL, "1"]
    required: true
    immutable: true
    description: The stock ledger unit. Use 1 for a count of individual consumables.
  low_stock_at:
    type: float
    label: Low-stock threshold
    required: true
    min: 0
```

- The item's [`unit`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue.unit) is a required,
  immutable enum that gives its ledger unit, and its
  [`threshold`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue.threshold) a required number
  with a minimum of zero or more.
- Each [lot](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue.lots) and
  [order](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue.orders) refers to an active catalogue
  item owned by the same project, with a required, immutable reference. Every concrete lot type's
  amount uses that reference as its `material`, and matches the item's unit.
- An open order counts its ordered packs less the packs already received; a missing received count
  means none.

A project's reader can page through `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/stock`, an item's
`/stock/{item_id}/lots` and the project's `/stock/orders`:

- Physical stock is the current ledger balance of the item's lots. Stored stock is in a container
  and checked in. Available stock also passes routine draw guards and hold, archive and expiry
  checks.
- A reader sees a container's ID and position only when they can read it.
- The low-stock list (`low_only=true`) has the items whose available stock is at or below their
  threshold, including items without lots. The periodic stock check tells the
  [`notify`](/reference/plugin-spec/entity-type/#stock_catalogue.notify) lab roles in the project
  once each time an item runs low.
