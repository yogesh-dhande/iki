# 0211. Lots, inventory, samples and requests have separate owners

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Service fields: production, sequencing and analytical fields and checks are each
  service's own request and result types, under [ADR 0235](0235-a-services-own-request-types-carry-its-fields.md).
- Connector distribution: the connector described below now ships as the [`acme/lims_connector` author example](../../examples/plugins/lims_connector/README.md) under [ADR 0218](0218-first-party-plugins-and-author-examples.md).

## Context

A lab that wants freezer locations currently installs a protein LIMS connector, supplies its host setting and gets testing, donor consent, instrument booking and service requests. Six other plugins take this dependency. A more important ambiguity shows up at the bench. A tube has a location and a balance, a batch has a disposition, and a portion taken for a particular test has observations. Today both the lot and the sample can purport to release the material.

## Decision

| Concept   | What its record means                                              | What it owns                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lot       | One batch of material made or bought together                      | Material identity, batch characterization, concentration, purity, expiry, specification and QA disposition. `iki/lots` owns the lot. `iki/qc_release` supplies specification, investigation and certificate workflows. |
| Inventory | A physical container and its place, whether full or empty          | `iki/inventory` owns Freezer, Shelf, Rack, Box and Tube. A filled Tube names its contents with a reference to `iki/core.Entity`. Its position, barcode, custody and amount use the core storage and ledger services.   |
| Sample    | A portion actually taken from a tube or lot for identifiable work  | `iki/samples.Sample` connects a source movement to the work, the people testing it and their measurements. The movement establishes what was taken, how much, by whom and when.                                        |
| Request   | One project's instruction to another team to do work on its behalf | `iki/requests` owns Request, private WorkItem and returned Deliverable types. The asking project owns the request. The serving project carries out the work and releases each deliverable.                             |

### Taking and testing a sample

Inventory can be installed by itself. A tube can hold a plasmid prep, a compound, cells or any other accessible record. Its `amount.follows` names its contents, so a lot's disposition, expiry and QA-sampling restrictions apply when drawing from its tube. The ledger identifies the physical tube used. Core `POST /entities/{ref}/aliquots` splits retained stock, creates portion records and records their common origin atomically. A split tube is still inventory. [ADR 0139](0139-a-records-amount-is-a-ledger-of-what-moved.md) · [ADR 0180](0180-where-material-is-and-how-its-received-moved-and-counted.md)

Use a draw for material committed to work. Create a pending sample with an immutable source (Tube or Lot) and an identified purpose. Requests and plates can be referenced as records. For a run, record its run identifier and use its input provenance when it executes. The material-use purpose on the ledger (routine or QA sampling) has a separate meaning. Draw from the source with the sample as `used_in`. Its computed `draws_received` receipt pins the movement, source record and revision, normalized quantity and unit, operator and time. A tube draw also requires its scan. A direct lot draw keeps the lot's native units and eligibility rules. The sample acquires no second stock balance. [ADR 0189](0189-an-experiment-pins-the-evidence-its-witness-signs.md) · [ADR 0193](0193-a-stability-pull-keeps-its-protocol-revision-and-ledger-draw.md)

This is the reviewed sample's procedure. A plain sample is completed by its analyst without a signed review ([ADR 0236](0236-a-lab-chooses-each-samples-testing-procedure.md)). The pending sample accepts one draw from its assigned source. Receipt and debit commit together. Admission to Received requires that receipt. In testing, the analyst records measurement rows on the sample, with the assay or method, observed value, unit where applicable and notes. The analyst signs submission for review. The reviewer signs the move to Reviewed and must be independent of the submitter and the people who edited the measurements in this cycle. The independent signature completes the sample in Reviewed. A correction to reviewed results reopens testing with a reason and requires fresh authorship and review signatures. My work follows the analyst and then the reviewer. Protein-specific thresholds, pass/fail disposition and QA release belong with the lot. [ADR 0126](0126-an-electronic-signature-is-bound-to-the-revision-it-signs.md) · [ADR 0179](0179-a-corrected-result-goes-back-for-review.md) · [ADR 0192](0192-lot-release-testing-pins-a-specification-and-issues-a-signed-certificate.md)

### Sending work to another team

The Request family declares its shared intake contract once. For example, `sends: [records, materials, tubes]` shares selected project records, physical material records and expected storage tubes. `materials: [materials, tubes]` identifies material available to the work. `receives.field: tubes` lets a coordinator reconcile a shipment. Empty lists allow a request for work with no shipment. Priority, assignee and queue status can remain visible on the request. Internal scheduling detail and working notes belong on one or more service-owned WorkItems. A released Deliverable carries the report, outcome and evidence the requester can read. [ADR 0132](0132-a-request-is-owned-by-the-project-that-asks-and-served-by-another.md) · [ADR 0149](0149-a-result-leaves-its-service-only-by-release.md)

Request and Deliverable are abstract parents. Plain, Research and QC are their concrete subtypes. Each concrete deliverable declares its own immutable request reference and release condition. Plain delivery is an explicit locked return. Research requires signed review. QC requires signed authorship, independent review and independent QA release. The paired reference prevents an unsigned plain deliverable from answering a QC request. Each deliverable has one immutable material reference for its subject, such as a tested sample or a sequencing tube. The decision to mark a request delivered uses the actual releases and their coverage of each commissioned subject. Production, sequencing and analytical work can retain evidence checks for what was requested. An arbitrary collaboration can leave those service-specific fields empty. [ADR 0195](0195-core-facility-requests-return-signed-deliverables.md)

When a deliverable names an `iki/samples.Sample` or a subtype, its assigned `source` describes the planned withdrawal. Coverage of that source begins with the recorded draw. The `DRAWN` movement names the sample in `used_in` and the tube or lot whose balance was debited in `entity`. A draw from an aliquot follows its split history back to the commissioned tube, including through further splits. A draw directly from a commissioned lot covers that lot.

ADR 0132 makes `sends` a declaration on the type. Subtypes inherit the declaration. A plugin needing different shared fields declares another request type with its own `request` block and declares deliverables that answer it. A requester selects records for the declared fields. Neither participant can choose additional fields to expose at runtime.

### Roles, holds, instruments and integration

- `iki/lab_roles` declares exactly `coordinator`, `engineer`, `analyst`, `reviewer` and `qa` (Sample and result QA). Consumers use qualified role names. Grants remain project-scoped. Lot QA and other domain-specific qualifications retain their own scope. [ADR 0116](0116-lab-roles-are-declared-by-plugins-and-given-per-project.md) · [ADR 0210](0210-delegated-actions-require-a-shared-lab-role.md)
- Inventory owns `hold_withdrawn`. The actionable fact is that identified stock was withdrawn from use. A person places a reasoned hold on the source tube. A project can enable a trigger to put its downstream tubes on hold, including portions made by core ledger splits or transfers. The action preserves existing reasons, can be retried and only sets holds. A person supplies a reason to clear one. A tube draw guard enforces the hold at withdrawal. The old donor-consent event, Donor type and consent-specific inputs retire together. This keeps inventory independent of a particular reason for withdrawal. Lot QA continues to control lot disposition.
- `iki/equipment` owns Instrument, Booking and the booking calendar alongside calibration, instrument use and impact assessment. There is one instrument reference from a booking through to the calibration history. [ADR 0194](0194-equipment-use-pins-calibration-and-failures-open-impact-cases.md)
- `iki/lims_connector_example` depends on Protein and adds its unique `lims_id`. It owns import, write-back, the nightly schedule, the rename trigger and the example API client. The README explains how to copy the pattern for keeping Iki in sync with an external system, including its host-bound egress and token. The general plugin template stays minimal.

## Consequences

A storage-only deployment can run without Protein or an external LIMS. A tester can trace a measurement to an actual debit. The owner of a lot can distinguish reviewed observations from the decision to release the batch. A team can receive a request without sharing the work still in progress.

The split changes plugin names, role grants, type names, component routes, dependency manifests, generated migrations and the dev deployment. This is a pre-M1 cutover on a fresh database. The implementation order, affected paths and acceptance tests are in the [migration plan](../plugin-split-plan.md).
