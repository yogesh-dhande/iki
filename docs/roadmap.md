# Roadmap to M1

Iki reaches its first release, M1, in the steps below. Each commit is a
complete slice, like a pull request. It has its tests, docs and UI states,
passes CI on its own, and leaves `main` deployable. Larger steps take several
commits. Steps marked with a letter, such as 34c, were added along the way; steps
after 50 were added once the first plan's 50 were nearly done.

This page lists every step, what it delivers, and the commits that did it
(`git show <commit>`). Architecture decisions are in [the ADRs](adr/README.md).

Status: **done**, **in progress**, **planned**, **deferred** (optional plugins wait
until after M1), or **to scope** (after M1, once its scope is agreed).
Plugins before M1 are built out fully. The workbenches that replace desktop tools (PyMOL, Geneious, ChemDraw) come after it: until then, people download a file, work on it in their desktop tool, and upload the result as a new revision.
