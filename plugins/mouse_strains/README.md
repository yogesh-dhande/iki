# Mouse strains

Register strains using Mouse Genome Informatics (MGI), or by the lab's own ID for a strain MGI doesn't list. Follow their repository links and find the cryopreserved material from each strain. A genotype record identifies the local animal or embryo group whose genotype was reported. A cryostock represents one physical carrier of embryos or sperm.

## Register a strain

1. In the project's actions, choose **Register a mouse strain from an MGI report**. Upload the headerless, tab-delimited `MGI_Strain.rpt` or an excerpt preserving its three columns, and enter the MGI accession. The action copies the official designation and retains the file, accession and digest of the matching row. The included two-row report contains C57BL/6J, accession `MGI:3028467`. The report lists no repository stocks: add the strain's JAX stock number, `000664`, from the JAX catalogue on the strain itself, where it links to its JAX page. [MGI report catalogue](https://www.informatics.jax.org/downloads/reports/index.html) · [JAX C57BL/6J](https://www.jax.org/strain/000664)
2. You can also create a **Mouse strain** directly. For example, the catalogue strain `B6(Cg)-Tyr<c-2J>/J` has MGI accession `MGI:2163869` and JAX stock `000058`. The MGI accession identifies the strain across projects in the same registry. Record stocks at other repositories, such as MMRRC or EMMA, as `REPOSITORY:ID`. [MGI strain entry](https://www.informatics.jax.org/strain/MGI:2163869) · [JAX stock 000058](https://www.jax.org/strain/000058)
3. For a strain MGI doesn't list, such as an in-house cross or a new founder line, set **Registered in** to `local` and enter the lab's own strain ID. That ID identifies the strain across the lab's projects. Give its designation in the lab's notation, following the MGI nomenclature rules when the strain may be published. [MGI nomenclature](https://www.informatics.jax.org/nomen/)
4. Register **Mouse allele** `MGI:1855985` with symbol `Tyr<c-2J>` and add it to the strain's **Alleles**. The list can contain the subset of catalogued alleles of interest. The strain designation, JAX stock and curated alleles are registry facts. A registrar can correct them with a reason. [MGI allele entry](https://www.informatics.jax.org/allele/MGI:1855985)

## Bank a carrier and trace its use

The following identifiers and observations describe a synthetic local inventory using the public albino B6 strain above.

1. Create a **Mouse genotype** for local subject `Example donor A`. Select its strain, enter the reported notation, for example `Tyr<c-2J>/Tyr<c-2J>`, choose its registered alleles and attach the original genotyping report. Use the notation in the report to preserve zygosity at each reported locus. [MGI catalogue genotype example](https://www.informatics.jax.org/allele/allgenoviews/MGI:1855985)
2. Create a **Mouse cryostock** for each carrier. Supply the kind, the carrier (a straw or a cryovial), strain, actual bank date and any printed barcode. An embryo carrier can record the integer number of embryos originally banked and their genotype if known. A sperm carrier can link to its donor's genotype. Leave the genotype empty when evidence is unavailable.
3. Create a **Mouse cryobox** with the positions of the holder in use: a cryovial box's rows and columns, or a goblet or cassette of straws as one row of its slots. From **Storage**, put it into a rack, a shelf, a dewar canister's rack or the dewar itself, and assign each carrier to a position. The same box can hold other containers, such as tubes of the donors' tissue. Scan its barcode or Iki HRID for checkout, return and identification. The location history records moves and custody changes.
4. To link the carrier's use to its result, enter an opening amount in µL: the measured suspension volume, or a straw's filled volume. For a withdrawal, scan the carrier, record the volume used and select the resulting record in **Used in**. A straw is thawed whole, so draw its whole volume. A cryovial's suspension can be drawn in part. A carrier is never split or poured into another, so each result keeps the carrier it came from. The ledger shows what remains and the graph links the result to the physical carrier. The embryo count at banking remains a historical observation.
5. Open **Upstream** on the result to trace it to the carrier, its strain, any recorded genotype and the registered alleles. **Downstream** on a strain finds the material and data made from it. The project's **Files** view can filter by a cryostock or genotype holding an original file.

The plugin uses the standard record, storage, amount and graph screens. Its storage dependencies are the box and container types in `iki/inventory`. [Local strains and carriers](../../docs/adr/0262-a-mouse-strain-can-be-the-labs-own-and-a-carrier-says-what-it-is.md)
